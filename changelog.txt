---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.0.1
Date: 2026-11-07
  Features:
    - First release on the mod portal.
    - Writes the game state for TouchDeck to script-output/touchdeck-bridge/ (state.json once per second, at once on events and commands): game time, speed, pause, research and progress, research suggestions, rockets, evolution, players, planet or space platform.
    - Power of the electric network you stand in, split by source (steam, turbines, solar, accumulators, fusion …) and by consumer.
    - Production and consumption per minute of every item and fluid on your surface (items.json).
    - Names in your language (names.txt, labels.txt).
    - Events: research finished and started, rocket launched, player died, evolution passed a 10 % step, command results.
    - Key bindings (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+…) for pause, speed, save, screenshot, research suggestions, cancel research, back to the player and refresh. Nothing to set up in Factorio.
    - Command field (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+B) and the /touchdeck chat command.
    - Optional support for UTLogistics: trains, deliveries, stations, networks, alerts and requests without a train, per planet; a "TouchDeck" section in the UTL station window to show a station on the TouchDeck and a "TouchDeck" tab in the UTL manager.
    - Works with and without Space Age.
  Locale:
    - English and German. French, Spanish, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Chinese (simplified) are AI translations – corrections are welcome.
